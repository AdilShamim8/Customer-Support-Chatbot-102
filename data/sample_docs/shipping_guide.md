# Shipping & Delivery Guide

*Effective: 2026-03-15 · Owner: Logistics · Doc ID: POL-SHP-002*

## Carrier Options at Checkout

| Method | Transit time | Cost | Tracking granularity |
|--------|-------------|------|----------------------|
| Standard | 4-7 business days | Free over $50, else $4.99 | Daily |
| Expedited | 2-3 business days | $9.99 | Daily |
| Express | Next business day (order by 2pm local) | $19.99 | Hourly during transit |
| International | 7-21 business days | Calculated at checkout | Daily, customs events included |

## Order Processing Timeline

1. **Order placed** → payment authorization (instant).
2. **Fulfillment** → picking and packing (1-2 business days; same-day
   for orders before 11am in-stock at your nearest warehouse).
3. **Handoff to carrier** → tracking number becomes active within
   4 hours of the first scan.
4. **In transit** → carrier updates; weather and peak-season delays
   appear as banners on the tracking page.

## Delayed or Stuck Shipments

- **No tracking movement for 48+ hours**: contact us - we open a trace
  with the carrier and, if unresolved within 3 business days, we reship
  free or refund in full.
- **Marked "delivered" but not received**: check mailrooms, neighbors,
  and porches first; then report within 7 days for our delivered-not-
  received investigation (photo evidence from the carrier speeds it up).
- **Peak season (Nov-Dec)**: add 2-4 days to all estimates; the order
  page shows a live-adjusted promise date during this window.

## Address Changes and Redirection

Address changes are possible while the order is in "fulfillment" state -
after carrier handoff, redirection must be arranged with the carrier
directly (their policies differ). Two failed delivery attempts return
the package to us; re-shipping then costs standard rates.

## Customs and Duties (International)

Import duties and taxes are the recipient's responsibility and are NOT
included in checkout prices for DDU destinations. DDP destinations show
all-in pricing at checkout. Customs holds beyond 14 days usually need a
response from the recipient - watch for the carrier's broker notice.
