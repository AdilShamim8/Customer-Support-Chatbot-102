# Product Warranty Terms

*Effective: 2026-01-01 · Owner: Quality Assurance · Doc ID: POL-WAR-004*

## Standard Warranty Coverage

| Product category | Warranty length | Covers | Does not cover |
|------------------|-----------------|--------|----------------|
| Electronics (audio, wearables, hubs) | 12 months | Manufacturing defects, battery below 60% capacity | Accidental damage, liquid exposure without IP rating |
| Cables & adapters | 24 months | Connectivity failures, insulation cracks | Pet damage, bent-by-force connectors |
| Home appliances | 24 months | Defects, motor failures | Cosmetic wear, user-replaceable parts after DIY repair |
| Refurbished items | 90 days | Same as new, prorated | Prior cosmetic condition (documented at sale) |

The warranty starts on the **delivery date** (not the order date) -
visible on your order page as "Warranty active through".

## Making a Warranty Claim

1. Have ready: order number, the item's serial (or photos of it), and
   a description (photos/videos of the defect dramatically speed this up).
2. Open **Account → Orders → Warranty claim** or start a chat with
   "warranty claim for order #XXXX".
3. Triage outcome within 1 business day:
   - **Repair** (rare - only for premium appliances): prepaid inbound
     shipping, 7-10 day turnaround.
   - **Replacement**: cross-shipped (we send first) for items under
     6 months old; standard exchange otherwise.
   - **Refund**: prorated by warranty time remaining, if a replacement
     is unavailable.

## AcmeCare+ (Extended Coverage)

Extends the standard warranty to 24-36 months and adds accidental
damage (drops, spills) with a $25 service fee per claim, max 2 claims
per year. Purchase is only possible within 60 days of the original
delivery; it attaches to the item, not the owner (transfers with
resale, one time).

## What Voids the Warranty

- Unauthorized disassembly (including third-party battery swaps on
  sealed units)
- Water damage on non-rated devices
- Firmware modified beyond official releases
- Serial number removed or defaced

## Warranty + Returns Interaction

Within the first 30 days, a defect routes through the DOA return path
(full refund/replacement, no fees). After 30 days, warranty terms
apply. There is never a case where you pay shipping for a confirmed
defect - if anyone quotes you a fee, escalate the chat.
