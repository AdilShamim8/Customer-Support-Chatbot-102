# Return & Refund Policy

*Effective: 2026-01-01 · Owner: Customer Experience · Doc ID: POL-RET-001*

## Standard Returns

Items purchased directly from our store can be returned within **30 days
of delivery** in their original condition and packaging.

| Condition | Window | Refund method |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| Unopened, original packaging | 30 days | Original payment method |
| Opened, resalable condition | 30 days | Original payment method, minus 15% restocking fee |
| Opened, non-resalable | Not returnable | Store credit exception at manager discretion |
| Defective on arrival (DOA) | 30 days (extended to 90 with photos) | Full refund or replacement, shipping on us |

## How to Start a Return

1. Open **Account → Orders → Return Item** next to the order line.
2. Print the prepaid label (domestic returns are free; international
   returns deduct actual shipping from the refund).
3. Drop the package at any partnered carrier location.

Refunds are issued within **2 business days** of the return being scanned
at our warehouse; banks typically post them in 3-5 additional days.

## Non-Returnable Categories

- Gift cards and digital licenses (activated)
- Personal-care items once unsealed
- Custom or engraved products
- Clearance items marked "final sale"

## Warranty Claims

Manufacturing defects within the warranty window (see the warranty card
in your box - typically 12 months for electronics) are repaired or
replaced at no cost. Accidental damage is covered by AcmeCare+ if active.

## Common Questions

**"My 30-day window just expired - any grace?"**
We apply a 5-day grace period for returns initiated in-app, as a
goodwill gesture. Beyond that, support agents may offer store credit
at their discretion for VIP-tier customers.

**"Do I pay return shipping for defective items?"**
No. Defective-item returns use a separate label with shipping paid by
us, and the restocking fee never applies to defect cases.

**"Can I exchange instead of refund?"**
Yes - exchanges for a different size or color are free within 30 days.
Exchanges for a different product are processed as refund + new order.
